Lab 2: Multivariate Visualization with D3

This visualization compares 12 cities across population, temperature, development level, and region.

X is population. Y is average temperature. Circle size is development level. Color is region. Hover a point for the city name and exact values.

Design Justification

I put population on the x-axis and temperature on the y-axis. Population is a ratio, so a linear x-axis starting at 0 makes city size easy to compare. Temperature is interval, so the y-axis keeps the gaps between 8°C and 26°C intact. Development is ordinal (Low, Medium, High), so I mapped it to circle radius: Low is smallest, High is largest. Region is a category, so each region has its own color. Hovering a point shows the city name and the raw numbers. The chart shows a downward slope: larger cities tend to be cooler and more developed, while the small southern cities sit high on the temperature axis with small circles.
